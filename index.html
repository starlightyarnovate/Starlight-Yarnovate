The Studio

A small online school for a slow, joyful craft

Starlight Yarnovate is an online crochet teaching studio. There are no crowded classrooms and no rushed videos here — you learn each stitch properly, practise with purpose, and build pieces one row at a time.

If you have never held a hook, you are in the right place. If you already crochet, you will sharpen your tension, finishing, and pattern reading.
