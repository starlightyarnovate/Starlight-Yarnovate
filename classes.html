Starting Points

Choose your thread

I

Beginnings — your first loops

For those who have never crocheted. You will get comfortable with the hook, the yarn, and the foundational movements everything else builds on.

  • Holding hook & yarn without strain
  • Slip knots, chains & first rows
  • Keeping edges straight from day one
II

Building — confident stitches

For makers with a little experience. You will even out your tension, learn core stitch families, and start reading simple patterns.

  • Core stitches & stitch combinations
  • Even tension & neat turning
  • Simple pattern reading
III

Making — finished pieces

For steady hands ready to complete work. You will shape pieces, join motifs, and finish edges cleanly.

  • Shaping & joining techniques
  • Edgings & clean finishing
  • Planning small complete projects

These descriptions outline general focus areas only. No specific curriculum, lesson count, live-session format, or materials list is promised here — confirm everything when you enquire.
